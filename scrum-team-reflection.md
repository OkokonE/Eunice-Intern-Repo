### Research & Learn

1. What are the key roles in a Scrum team, and where does QA fit in?
Key roles include:
- Scrum Master
- QA tester
- Product manager
- Developer

QA is part of the team that helps ensure the product works correctly and meets users' needs. Testers find bugs, check features, and work with developers to improve quality throughout development.

2. What happens in a Sprint Planning, Daily Standup, Sprint Review, and Retrospective?
- Sprint Planning: The team decides what work to do during the sprint. QA asks questions about requirements and plans how to test the features.
- Daily Standup: Team members briefly share their progress, what they plan to do next, and any blockers.
- Sprint Review: The team shows what it has completed and gets feedback from stakeholders. QA can share testing results and any known issues.
- Sprint Retrospective: The team discusses what went well, what went wrong, and how to improve the next sprint. QA can suggest ways to make testing more effective.

3. How does QA help the team maintain quality throughout the sprint?
QA helps by testing features as soon as they are ready instead of waiting until the end of the sprint.

4. What is shift-left testing, and why is early involvement of QA important?
Shift-left testing means starting testing activities earlier in the development process rather than waiting until the software is almost finished.
For example, before a developer builds a new reminder feature in Focus Bear, I could review the requirements and ask questions about when reminders should appear, I can also begin to write the test cases.

This helps identify misunderstandings early, reduces expensive rework, and gives the team more time to fix bugs before release.

5. How do testers collaborate with developers, product managers, and designers in Scrum?
- With developers: Explain bugs clearly, provide steps to reproduce them, and retest fixes.
- With product managers: Clarify requirements, understand which features are most important, and discuss the impact of bugs.
- With designers: Check whether the app matches the design and is easy to use, and report layout or usability problems.


### Reflection

1. How should QA prepare for a Sprint Planning meeting?
QA should review the requirements and user stories before the meeting, identify what needs to be tested, note questions to ask about anything unclear, assess possible risks, and estimate the testing effort. They should also consider the test data and environment needed.

2. What information should testers share in a Daily Standup?
- What they did yesterday
- What they plan to do today
- Blockers

While interning previously, durung daily stand-up we shared what was done the previous day, what will be done today and if any blockers appeared.

3. If a feature is about to be released, but QA finds a critical bug, what should happen next?
QA should immediately report the bug to the developers and Product Owner, explaining its impact and providing evidence. The team should assess the risk and decide whether to fix the bug before release or delay the release. The fix must be tested, along with relevant regression tests, before the feature is released.
