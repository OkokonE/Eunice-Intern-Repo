### Reflections

**Mission:** Help people build productive routines, block distractions, and focus on what matters without burning out. The company emphasizes supporting people with ADHD and autistic ADHD (AuDHD) through practical, compassionate productivity tools. 

**Vision:** The company’s manifesto expresses a vision of helping people protect their attention, creativity, wellbeing, and ability to do meaningful deep work. It aims to make productivity more sustainable through routines, intentional breaks, adequate rest, and technology designed around how people actually work. This is a synthesis of the manifesto rather than a separate formal vision statement. 

1. Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
Focus Bear was created to help people who struggle with focus and easily become overwhelmed overcome distractions, build healthy habits and routines, and take regular breaks to prevent burnout.

2. What problem is Focus Bear solving?
Focus Bear addresses the difficulty of staying focused and completing tasks or tapping into creativity due to distractions. It also helps people prevent burnout and develop healthy routines.

3. Why do you think this mission is important?
I believe this mission is important because it addresses real challenges that people face, especially those who are often misunderstood and sometimes labelled as underperforming or unserious.

4. How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?
One of my greatest desires is to help people. Working with the Focus Bear team to develop solutions that help people overcome their challenges aligns with this desire.

5. Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?
I easily get distracted, but I can also become hyperfocused once I find my rhythm while working.


Do you know someone with ADHD or autism or even someone without a diagnosis who has struggled socially or to perform well at uni? How might Focus Bear help them?
In Nigeria, ADHD is not a widely recognised term or diagnosis, and I believe many people may be living with it without knowing. I personally do not know anyone who has been diagnosed with ADHD, but my sister has been and still struggles with her academics sometimes.

I believe Focus Bear could help her by blocking distractions on her phone, especially when she is studying. It could also help her develop healthy habits and routines that enable her to stay focused and perform better academically.
