### Reflections

Freedom productivity app:
From my research, I learnt that the Freedom productivity app focuses on blocking distractions during work sessions. It allows users to create block lists and use Locked Mode to prevent themselves from ending sessions early. It also has a feature that blocks internet browsing, while still allowing installed applications to access the internet.

Tiimo productivity app:
From my research, I learnt that Tiimo helps users create and track their habits and routines. It features a countdown timer for each task, incorporates AI to assist with planning, and allows users to switch between user profiles.

Cold Turkey productivity app:
Emphasises strict, difficult-to-bypass blocking and computer-level restrictions.

Routinery productivity app:
Focuses on helping users build consistent daily routines using timed steps, reminders, and progress tracking. It's especially useful for structuring activities such as morning preparations, study sessions, and bedtime routines.


1. What makes Focus Bear different from these apps?
Focus Bear’s key difference is that it combines two functions in one app: habit and routine building, and distraction blocking during focus sessions. The other apps researched tend to concentrate more heavily on one side of productivity.
Our distinctive strength is the integration of guided routines, work-session distraction blocking, and breaks into one productivity system.

2. If you were a user, why would you choose Focus Bear over competitors?
If I were a user, I would choose Focus Bear because it offers several useful productivity features in one app. It helps users block distractions, build healthy habits and routines, and perform simple exercises during breaks. I also like that its free version offers useful features, such as website and app blocking, routines, microbreaks, and a to-do list. This makes it a practical option for people who want to improve their productivity and develop healthier work habits without immediately paying for a subscription.

3. What’s one feature that other apps have that Focus Bear doesn’t?
One feature that other apps have that Focus Bear does not offer is the ability to switch between multiple user profiles, as seen in Tiimo. This would be useful for people sharing an account but needing separate routines and schedules.

4. Based on your research, what’s one improvement you think Focus Bear could make?
Based on my research, I think Focus Bear could improve its account settings by displaying all devices currently using the account. This would help users keep track of their connected devices, easily identify where their account is active, and manage their account security more effectively. It would also make the cross-device experience more transparent and user-friendly.
